Positive Reinforcement Club

Experimentation · personal project

I’ve been experimenting with the idea of turning self-care into a loyalty program. Each punch card is a tiny nudge toward habits that make me feel more grounded, creative, or connected.

The Positive Reinforcement Club started as a typography and color play project — but it’s also become a fun, visual way to motivate myself (and my family) to do the things that make us feel good.

Punching towards progress (not perfection)
